Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

The short version

Lähdöt doesn't collect, store or share personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party trackers.

What stays on your device

The stops and settings you choose are stored only on your device and shared between the app and its widget.

Location

Only if you tap the location button when adding a stop, Lähdöt asks iOS for permission and uses your current position once to centre the map. It isn't saved, sent to me or used for anything else. You can change the permission any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Lähdöt.

Network requests

To show departures, the app requests data for your chosen stops from HSL's Digitransit API. As with any internet request, this reveals your IP address to that service. The map is shown with Apple Maps, which is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Apple's App Store statistics

Apple may give developers aggregated, anonymous statistics from users who chose to share them. I can't identify individual users from them.

Changes

If this changes, I'll update this page and the date above.
