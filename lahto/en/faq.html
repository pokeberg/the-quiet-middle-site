Frequently asked questions

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, tap +, search for Lähdöt and pick a size.

The widget isn't updating, or the times look old.

iOS decides how often widgets refresh, so a widget can lag a few minutes behind the app. Open Lähdöt to refresh. Times are real-time when HSL provides real-time data, otherwise they follow the timetable.

Which areas does it cover?

The HSL region: Helsinki, Espoo, Vantaa and the other HSL-area municipalities.

Why does the app ask for my location?

Only to centre the map when you add a stop. It's optional; you can move the map yourself.

Do you collect my data?

No. See the privacy policy for details.

Which iOS version do I need?

iOS 17 or later.

I found a problem. How do I report it?

Send me an email and include your iOS version and the name of the stop.
